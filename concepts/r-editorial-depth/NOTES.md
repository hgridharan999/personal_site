**(a) Mark.** A small tip, an ink waterline, a larger faceted hull, and Hari's own hiker (mirrored to face the peak, never redrawn) on the ledge beside the tip. One `<symbol id="mk">`, two inks: ink on white in the header (hiker via `brightness(0)`), ice on blue in the footer (cream lines untouched).

**(b) Favicon.** `favicon.svg`: near-ink tile, white tip over an ice waterline, blue faceted hull, hiker cut to head, body and pole. At 16px it holds as peak, line, blue mass.

**(c) Voice.** One line, set on the waterline: "The résumé ends here. The rest is below."

**(d) System.**
- Type: Fraunces 300 (pinned SOFT 100, WONK 1, optical size free; about 74 KB) for lockup, headlines, old-style numerals, readings. Hanken Grotesk for body and labels as scaled capitals (600, +0.2em). DM Mono for data only.
- Color: above `#FFF / #111 / #0F7DAA`. Waterline: 1.25px ink hairline with a blue echo. Below: `#E3F3F9` to `#0D6A92` by 218px (white 6.0:1, palest text 5.0:1), then `#08435F`, then `#0A1822`.
- Motion: slow; stills under reduced motion. The outline draws only as far as you have scrolled; the waterline answers scroll speed and touch.

**(e) Ownable.** One 1.2px line from the sketched peak to the keel, kinking into the hull at the waterline, with ruler readings on every label (+9 m peak, 0, -81 m keel: the iceberg's 1:9, not pixels). Specimens are numbered plates, Fig. 1 to 6. The seabed is Quandary read as bathymetry, his GPS track the one bright thread.

**(f) Cut first.** The live depth gauge at the line's nib, then the facet chords, then the spice specimen.

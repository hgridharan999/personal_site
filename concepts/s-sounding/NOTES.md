# Sounding: brand system

**Idea.** The site is a depth-sounding instrument. Every label carries a reading: +0.50 KM at the top, 0.00 at the waterline, -4.50 KM at the seabed (10% above, 90% below). Above water is the instrument's clean face; below, each personal section is a different reading on the same deep-blue screen.

**(a) Mark.** Hari's hiker art (mirrored, black above water) stands on a hairline waterline; three sonar arcs ping below his feet. Built from `hiker.webp` plus one SVG (line and three arcs, iceberg blue). The device returns at the waterline as the transducer (triangle over line over arcs), where the ping starts.

**(b) Favicon.** `favicon.svg`: a pictogram of the same pose (pack, pole, lean) on a #0A3550 tile, ice waterline, two arcs. Checked at 16 px. `mark.svg` is the standalone version. The scratchy art dies below about 40 px, so the small mark is drawn, not traced.

**(c) Voice.** No tagline. The instrument speaks: "SURFACE", "SOUNDING BEGINS".

**(d) System.** Hanken Grotesk text, Instrument Serif italic for "Hari", DM Mono for every reading.
Ramp: white, #111, #0F7DAA above; a #0F7DAA hairline at the waterline; #0A5578 down to #031420 below, text #F2FBFE, labels #A9D9EB, lines #7FD3EE (at least 5.3:1 on the shallowest water).
Motion: one ping; each plate resolves once with a sweep (text never waits); then one plate at a time idles, paused off-screen. Reduced motion gets finished stills.

**(e) Ownable.** Readings on every label, the ping, the plate system, real terrain scanned in the brand's own language.

**(f) Cut first.** The depth-gauge tape in the margin (the most dashboard-like piece), then the spectrogram.

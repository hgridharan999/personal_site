# Hiker mark notes

**Recommendation: B, Field sketch** in the header, with **D, Glyph** as the favicon. Field sketch stays closest to the scratchy hiker people already know: hood up, rolled pack lid, fist on the front pole, trailing pole. Its twelve overshooting strokes draw like a real pen and sit well beside the lockup. Runner-up: A, Continuous line, cleaner and more logo-like.

## At 32 px

- **A Continuous line:** still a hooded figure with a pack and two poles. The fist and legs blur.
- **B Field sketch:** the same, anchored by its ground stroke. Strokes fall below 1 px, so it reads light.
- **C Ridge:** the weakest. The hiker is about 20 px tall and the contours fade to haze.
- **D Glyph:** built for this size. A generic hiker pictogram, trading identity for legibility.
- **E Traced:** readable, but thin and lumpy.

## Trace caveats

The outline against the sky is clean. The legs sit on ground hatching, so I filtered out near-horizontal ink and hand-tuned one threshold patch. The poles are straight lines fitted to the image. The strap and leg lines wobble, and the boots merge into a lump.

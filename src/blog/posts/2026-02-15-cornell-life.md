Ithaca is cold in a way that feels personal. You walk to class and the wind comes straight off Cayuga Lake like it has a grievance with you specifically. I didn't expect to love it as much as I do.

## The Part Everyone Warns You About

Everyone said "don't overcommit freshman year." I joined Cornell DTI, the Entrepreneurship Club, and co-founded a startup. So that went well.

The honest version: it was too much at first, then it wasn't. The startup — a prediction market platform — kept me grounded because it had real stakes. Either the thing works or it doesn't. That clarity is actually easier to work with than the ambient pressure of grades.

## What I'm Learning About ML

Dyson is primarily a business school but I've been taking CS and INFO courses that let me get into the technical side. BERT embeddings, semantic search, gradient boosted trees — these aren't just resume keywords anymore, they're tools I reach for when solving actual problems.

The climate tech project came out of wanting to apply those tools to something that felt meaningful rather than just impressive.

## The Climbing

There are no 14ers in upstate New York. The hiking is gentler but in a way that's made me appreciate it differently — less about summiting, more about being in the gorges. Taughannock Falls in February with ice formations is genuinely one of the better things I've seen.

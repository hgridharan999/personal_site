**Solipsism** — the philosophical idea that you, the individual, are the only 'real' person on earth, and that it is impossible to know if anyone else is real.

First off, as someone who only recently learned about this term, if it doesn't scare you at least a little bit, I think you might be a sociopath.

## The Unsettling Thought Experiment

Take a second and think about it. What if every single person around you isn't actually real? There's a saying that all reality is just one's perception (a saying that I quote a tad too often). But if that's true, how could we hope to possibly discern what the objective truth is? What if every person around you is either the equivalent of a hallucination or a ChatGPT wrapper?

Does that mean we can't trust a singular soul around us, or does that mean that everything that other people say is just a reinforcement of our own thoughts? There are so many ways to rationalize this belief, because, like so many other things in this universe, we simply **do not have enough answers** nor do we have the evidence to try to arrive at one.

## The Surprising Upside

While this concept seems somewhat dire (maybe this is why so many philosophers seem so sad), I choose to look at it slightly differently.

**I choose to look at it as rationale for why I should simply take every single risk I ever encounter.**

Because if the only thing that's holding me back is public opinion, then why should I even care — especially when that public itself may not even be real?

## The Real Barrier to Success

I think one of the greatest barriers to success is **inaction**. I think one of the greatest reasons behind inaction is paralysis of public opinion. But if we choose to buy into the fact that the only voice that matters at the end of the day is one's own, then why bother about the subjective reality of others on the objective reality of your own?

While I do realize that this convention of conversation and terraced thinking is a tad trivial, I also believe that with a bit more delusion and a bit more confidence in our lives, we'd end up doing way more than we could ever imagine.

So why get stuck in your own head? **Failing and getting stuck is worse than getting stuck before you even start.**

Take every single risk. Because why not.

date: "2026-06-29"

---

I've been trying to do a lot this summer.

Professionally, I'm doing **two internships** at once: operations at a mortgage startup and consulting at a Fortune 1000 firm. I'm also working on my startup, plugging [loadstonelabs.com](https://loadstonelabs.com) whenever I can.

Physically, I'm continuing to lean bulk, currently at **185 bench / 155 bw**, while hiking 14ers in my free time.

I'm also trying to enjoy the last summer being a kid, and connecting with people I haven't seen in a year, while also not destroying my sleep schedule.

Doing all of this in a time when I could alternatively just be lounging in bed all day has got me thinking again.

Today's been the first day in a couple of months that I've actually sat down and found both motivation and time to write down my thoughts.

Clearly, I've been stretching myself too thin. I myself know that.

But at the same time, it has forced me to constantly be in action, not sitting in inaction. That has removed the biggest barrier for me: **overthinking**.

I have made more progress with this startup in the last month than I did with any previous ventures in the last year. I've learned way too much about mortgage and loan officers and PPE engines. I'm in the greatest shape of my life. So by all standards, it's worked out pretty well.

However, this has also resulted in me neglecting other things.

I haven't spent as much time with family and friends as I wanted to. I have been less intentional about my actions, words, and overall gratitude. I have been less introspective, and more things have become routine rather than active choices.

Summary? I have made more progress, but that progress comes at the price of peace of mind.

I want to take this chance to be more intentional this summer, while also being more focused and productive than I have in my entire life.

That starts with committing to (at least) one blog post here per day, starting a substack, and jotting down at least something in my journal every day.

I believe that doing this alongside all of the "productive" other things will make the rest of this summer even better, especially considering all of the travel and experiences that are yet to come.

However, there is clearly some irony that I'm here, saying I'm stretched thin, and then vowing to do even more.

That's what leads to so much confusion for me, but also the only clarity I need.

There's always more that we can do in any aspect of our lives. It's just about choosing those things carefully and actually committing to the ones that matter.

For me, everything above does matter, at least for now.

So I'm going to continue all of it, because right now, it's the best path forward.

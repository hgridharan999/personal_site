**Neuroplasticity** — the ability of the brain to form and reorganize synaptic connections, especially in response to learning or experience. A reason why Bojack Horseman was wrong.

## The Pattern We Fall Into

It is so easy for someone to be stuck in a pattern of life and to be resistant to change. All of us want things to change in our lives, whether that's more success, company, or love, yet ironically resist the very changes that could make these hopes reality.

But the simple fact that we are **hardwired** to be capable of change means that we are constantly changing whether or not we see it. The real challenge comes in shaping how we are changed by what we experienced.

## What This Means to Me

This means two things to me.

**One:** I should never stop pursuing whatever I want, because only growth and change can come out of it. This means that I continue doing the things I love and especially the things I know I should do, especially when I am demotivated or burnt out.

**Second:** I should never give up on my own ability to change for the better. It means that literally everyone has the capacity for growth at every stage and every moment of their life. That means being open to the possibility of growth at every corner, and that even if you suffer setback, positives are always attainable.

## Why Bojack Was Wrong

Neuroplasticity = a bit of proof that Bojack Horseman is wrong, because all of us can and will change.
